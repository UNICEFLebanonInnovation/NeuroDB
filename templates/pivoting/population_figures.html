Data Sources

LRP 2026 Population Package

Source for Lebanese:

  • Labour Force and Household Living Conditions Survey (LFHLCS) 2018–2019 (CAS, ILO).
  • IOM/DTM Round 87, As of 31 May 2025

Source for Syrians:

  • UNHCR Registration and Assistance Databases (as of 30 July 2025)
  • The updated number of Syrians in Lebanon reflects a change based on four factors:
    • (1) Individuals who have been inactivated from UNHCR’s registration records due to verified or presumed spontaneous\/unassisted return in 2025.
    • (2) Those who returned in 2025 through UNHCR’s facilitated return programme.
    • (3) Those expected to return and inactivated by mid-2026.
    • (4) New arrivals who entered Lebanon after December 2024.
  • The total number of displaced Syrians in Lebanon for 2026 is calculated after taking into account the number of Syrians who have returned to Syria, the number of Syrians estimated to return by mid-2026, the number of Syrians who were inactivated from UNHCR’s database, and the number of Syrians who newly arrived in Lebanon after December 8, 2024. The resulting total 1.12 million displaced Syrians includes 1 million displaced Syrians who have not yet returned to Syria and 120,000 newly arrived after December 8, 2024.

Source for Palestinians:

  • PRS data: UNRWA 2024 Digital Individual Verification Data
  • PRL data: UNRWA annual average service delivery reports (all services)

Notes:

  • Camps boundaries are defined according to the official administrative parcels which may slightly differs from UNRWA's operational areas. Adjacent Gatherings population (including those served by the agency) are categorized under gatherings.
  • Camp population mentioned here includes all nationalities and refugee status: PRL, PRS, Syrian, Lebanese and others.
  • A small number of Palestinian Households were found in areas of (koura, Rachaya, Hasbaya, Marjaayoun) . They were added to the closest gathering s for confidentiality.
  • The figures in this table are different from previously published counts as they are weighted to adjust for the estimated under-coverage rates found during the post enumeration exercise. Kindly note that the totals might slightly differ due to the rounding during the weighting operation.
  • The data reference date is July 2017. Population projection for the coming 10 years should be calculated, based on fertility and mortality rates as well as migration data.
  • Data aggregation, used here, according to Governerates geography do not match the Census key-findings and final reports aggregtion. (census data classifies for example Burj Barjneh in Beirut not Mount Lebanon)
  • *Migrants Data is not included in this sheet as it has different Sex and Age disaggregation
