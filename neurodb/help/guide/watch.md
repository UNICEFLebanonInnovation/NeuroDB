# For you and What's new

## For you (NeuroDB Watch)

[For you](/for-you/) (sidebar) is NeuroDB Watch: every morning at 07:45, and shortly after new data
arrives, it asks fixed questions of NeuroDB's own tables (rules, no AI): what falls due soon, what is
still open, how these things connect. It remembers each thing it follows from one day to the next and
tells each person once, at the right moment. The sidebar shows the number of points that need you today
and that you have not seen yet; the overview has a *For you* card.

The page shows, in order: today's note (your section's, and the whole country's for the country view),
*Needs you today* (at most 5 points), *Coming up (next 30 days)*, *How things connect* (open points that
meet on one partner or grant), *Good to know*, *Everything else open* and *How this works*. Each point
says why it is shown ("New", "Due in 3 days", "Got worse"...), how sure NeuroDB is (*Sure*, *Likely*,
*Please check*), how it knows (the source, when it was last synced, a link) and what it remembers.

### What it follows

- Partner progress reports due in the next 14 days and not submitted.
- Open action points due in the next 14 days, and normal-priority ones past their date.
- Programme documents ending in the next 60 days, and those ended in eTools and not closed.
- Grants expiring in the next 90 days with money unspent; funds reservations ending in the next 30 days
  with money outstanding.
- From October: partners whose HACT programmatic visits, spot checks or audits are below what is
  required.
- Agreed dates on daily review findings.
- **Field monitoring follow-up**: reported visits rated off track or constrained, ended 14 to 120 days
  ago, with no action point following them up (critical when off track and ended over 30 days ago).
- Indicators the year-end forecast says are likely to fall short (while the forecasts are shown).
- The critical and warning findings of the latest daily review.

New checks start in **trial**: their points go to the whole-country view only until an administrator
switches them on for staff.

### Who is told

Each person follows their own section. Administrators and the Management group also get the
whole-country view. You are told about a point again only when it gets worse, crosses one of its dates,
becomes overdue, a reminder ends, or it closes ("Resolved", "Date passed, not done", "No longer followed
here").

### Reactions

*Useful*, *Not useful*, *Done*, *Not mine*, *Remind me later*, *Something's wrong* (with an optional
comment only administrators read) and *Look into this* (opens Ask NeuroDB with the question typed). A
check that many people find not useful goes back to trial by itself.

### The AI in For you

ChatGPT writes only the short morning note (each sentence must cite the points it rests on, with the
same numbers and dates) and looks into at most 3 critical points a day with read-only look-ups. It never
decides what is followed, how serious it is, when it is due or who is told.

## What's new

[What's new](/whats-new/) (sidebar) lists what changed in any source: new things (a partner, programme
document, donor, grant, database, document...), changes (a status, end date, budget, risk rating, an
indicator's value), new or ended links (a programme document newly funded by a donor), and things gone.

- A change is **notable** when it is a new or gone thing of the main kinds, a status or date that
  changed, a figure that moved by at least 10% (and at least $1,000 for money), a funding or reporting
  link, or a critical review finding that appears or goes. *Include minor changes* shows the rest.
- A field monitoring visit is news when it comes in rated off track or constrained and dated (its start
  date, else its end date) in the last 30 days, or when its rating changes later.
- Filter by the last 24 hours, 7 or 30 days, section and kind. Each line links to the page of the thing.
- The daily note (07:30) sums up the notable changes of the last 24 hours, for everyone and per section.
