# Country overview, management brief and daily review

## The country overview

The [country overview](/country-overview/) shows every figure of the year: one page for the whole
intervention, read from the synced eTools tables. [Home](/help/home/) shows its five key figures and links
to it.

| Row | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Impact on children | Children reached (eTools partner reports), by governorate, achievement against target by section, monthly reach, coverage of the estimated children per governorate. |
| Value for money | Funds disbursed of reserved, cost per child reached by section, spending against delivery, funding by donor, partnerships that need a decision. |
| Delivery and assurance | Indicator status by section, assurance counts (field monitoring, TPM visits, action points, HACT risk), findings by rating, what needs attention. |
| Progress | TPM visits planned, completed and with the report overdue per month; action points due, closed and past due per month. |

- **Filters**: year (calendar years), section and governorate (click a tile). You land on your own
  section, with every section one click away; members of the Management group, and Administrators
  without a section, land on the whole country. A link from Home keeps Home's sections.
- **Which programme documents**: every one running in the selected year, whatever its status now,
  except drafts and cancelled ones.
- **Children reached**: from eTools partner reports, with the months they cover. Early in the year
  (January to April), until the first partner reports of the year come in, the tile says *Not yet* and
  links to last year.
- **One indicator per programme document**: the indicators of one programme document count the same
  children, so they are never added. Each programme document counts once, with its *unique
  beneficiaries indicator* (chosen on the programme document page by an Administrator or a Section
  editor of its section), else its *largest single indicator* (its largest children indicator that
  year). The eTools figure is the sum over the programme documents: partners may reach the same
  children. A value too large to be true is never counted; the page lists it so it can be checked.
  When the chosen indicator cannot count (its value is too large to be true, or it has become a
  percentage), the programme document page says so and its largest single indicator stands in.
  Reports submitted but not yet accepted count, and the tile says how many children come from them
  (a report sent back later lowers the figure).
- **Achievement against target**: the median share of the target reached by the children indicators of
  each section (each capped at 100%); targets of different indicators are never added.
- **Cost per child**: what the programme documents with a children result disbursed to date, over the
  children they reached in the year (each one's main indicator). Compare sections, not absolute values.
- **The page opens at once**: the filters and the strip at the bottom come first, with a grey box
  where each block of figures will go. The tiles, the charts and What's new then appear one after the
  other as they are ready, and you can use the page meanwhile. A block that takes long says "Still
  working on this part"; a block that could not load says so and has a **Try again** button, and the
  rest of the page keeps working. If you print before every block has appeared, a block still loading
  prints a short note instead: wait until the page is complete. The daily review and the For you card
  are on their own pages and on [Home](/help/home/).
- Figures are kept 2 minutes, so opening the same filter again soon is faster; the end of any sync
  shows at once. The strip at the bottom says when each source last changed.

## The management brief

The [management brief](/brief/) is the overview read for decisions, for senior management. It compares
the selected year (calendar years) with the year before, in eight tabs:

- **Brief**: five tiles with a comparison (children reached against the same months of last year,
  achievement against the elapsed period, disbursed of reserved, cost per child, indicators on track
  against 30 days ago), *Things to decide* (up to five decisions from the daily review, marked
  *AI-suggested*) and *Sections at a glance*. *Copy summary* copies the headline figures for minutes.
- **Ahead or behind**, **How sure** (confidence per section: High, Medium or Low, with what keeps it
  from High), **Who and where**, **Partners**, **Money**, **Action** and **Reference** (where the
  figures come from).
- Values that cannot be right (above the child population of Lebanon, or more than 20 times their
  target) are left out, and the section's confidence is Low.
- **Children reached** is compared like with like, over the same months of both years: up to the last
  month whose partner reports are all in (partners report every quarter, over several weeks: a quarter
  with only some of its reports in is left out until nine in ten have come). There is no comparison
  ("Not compared") against a year with no eTools partner reports or with reports for too few programme
  documents, while not even the first quarter's partner reports are all in, or for a year that has not
  started. Early in the year, before the first partner reports come in, the tile reads
  *Not yet*.
- **Where the year ends at this pace** draws children reached as reported (solid) to its last month and
  at this pace (dotted) to December, for the year in progress; a key under the chart says what the
  solid line, the dotted line and the shaded months to come are. A year that is over is not projected:
  its line ends at its last reported month.
- **The page opens at once**: the filters, the *Figures as of* line, the tabs and the *Reference*
  block come first, with a grey box where the figures will go. The *Brief* tab's tiles, decisions and
  sections at a glance fill in first, then the six other tabs together, with their charts; you can
  use the page meanwhile. A tab whose box has not filled in yet takes you there as soon as it has. A
  box that takes long says "Still working on this part"; a box that could not load says so and has a
  **Try again** button, and the rest of the page keeps working. Opened again within 2 minutes with
  the same filter, the page comes complete at once.
- **Print** waits for every box: the button reads "Preparing the page…" until every tab and its
  charts are drawn, then opens the print window (after a minute it prints what it has). Ctrl+P
  before the page is complete prints a short note in place of each box still loading.
- Figures are kept 2 minutes; the end of a sync, a new daily review, a section plan or a finding
  assignment entered in the administration, and a changed indicator flag show at once.

## The daily review

Every morning (at 06:00 unless an administrator changed its time) the daily review runs seventeen fixed
checks over the synced data and keeps the result as a dated review with its findings. It has a page of
its own, [Daily review](/daily-review/), which says the time it runs; Home shows its summary.

- Checks include indicators newly off track, indicators not reported, progress reports overdue,
  spending ahead of achievement, under-disbursed programme documents, programme documents ending soon
  below target, late TPM reports, overdue high-priority action points, field monitoring findings off
  track, the three newer checks below, and the data problems (failed syncs, data quality, stale
  sources), summed up in one line that links to Data health.
- Each finding is *new*, *still open* or *resolved* compared with the day before.
- With the AI set up, the AI writes four to six plain sentences from the day's findings only, and
  chooses up to five decisions for the management brief; a decision is kept only when every finding it
  cites exists and every number it writes is in those findings. Without the AI, a template writes the
  summary.
- Findings are prompts to look, never verdicts on partners or staff.

### The three newer checks

- **Field monitoring off track while the reports are on track.** A field monitoring visit in the last
  90 days rated a programme document off track, but the partner's reports put every one of its
  indicators with a target and a report on track or ahead of schedule. The finding lists the visits
  (reference, date, rating, and how the visit was matched to the programme document) and the
  indicators with their Reached and target. A visit is counted only when it names one programme
  document for sure; when its reference could mean two, it is left out. What to do: read the visit's
  findings in eTools next to the latest progress report, and ask the programme section which one is
  right.
- **Audit findings with no action point.** An audit, special audit or spot check whose final report
  came out more than 60 days ago (and in the last two years) lists findings, and no action point
  follows it up. It is a warning when there are high-priority findings, to note otherwise. It counts
  the action points and findings as the audit's own page does, and opens that page. What to do: agree
  the follow-up with the partner and raise the action points in eTools, or note there why none is
  needed.
- **HACT assurance still to do this year.** All year round, a partner whose programmatic visits, spot
  checks or audits done this year are below what HACT requires, with the months left. It reads the
  same figures as NeuroDB Watch, which has its own HACT check from 1 October, so the daily review keeps
  it to note. It opens the partner's page and links to the HACT visit plan. What to do: plan the visits,
  spot checks or audits still needed.

A new check starts in **trial**. Its findings show on the Daily review page and on Home with a *Trial*
mark, and they are not sent to NeuroDB Watch. An administrator looks at them, then switches the check on in the
administration (Data and sync, Daily review checks). From the next morning's review, its warnings
and critical findings also go to NeuroDB Watch. The older checks are on.
