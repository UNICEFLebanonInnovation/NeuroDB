{% trans "How the daily review works" %}

{% blocktrans %}Every morning, after the night's eTools sync, fourteen fixed checks read the programme data: indicators newly off track, indicators never reported, overdue progress reports, spending ahead of delivery, under-disbursed and ending programme documents, late TPM reports, overdue high-priority action points, off-track monitoring findings, failed syncs, data quality, unplaced locations, stale sources and improvements.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Each finding is compared with the day before (new, still open or resolved), ranked by severity and by the children behind target, and links to the page that holds the evidence. When the AI assistant is configured it writes the short summary from the findings alone; otherwise a template does.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}A finding is a prompt for a person to look, never a verdict on a partner or a colleague.{% endblocktrans %}
