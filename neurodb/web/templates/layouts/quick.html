{% comment %}The quick view of a page (web.quick, static/js/quickview.js): swapped into the shared window (components/modal.html, #modal-content), which is labelled by the title below (#modal-title). The header: Back to the quick view before (shown by the script once there is one), the page's title, Open full page (the page's own address) and Close. The body: the page's header line (app.css hides its breadcrumb and its title, which the window's header holds), its content and its parts, which load inside the window as on the page. Nothing of the page's head comes in but its stylesheets: the head_extra block is kept inert (a template), and the script adds its stylesheets to the page; a refresh it asks for (a document still being read) is not done in the window. The page's scripts block is left out: a page that needs its own script is not quick-viewable (web.quick.not_quick). No menu, top bar, messages or footer.{% endcomment %}
